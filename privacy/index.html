Chessformator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Chessformator is an offline chess-coaching app. It does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. The developer has no servers that receive information from the app.

Data stored on your device

Your profile, imported games, analysis and coaching history are stored locally on your device. They never leave it unless you export or share them yourself, for example as a PDF or PGN. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Microphone: optional voice input. Speech is transcribed on the device by Whisper, and the recording is deleted after transcription. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect to a Chessnut Move board and synchronise moves.

Optional model downloads

If you choose to download an analysis, voice or coaching model, the app fetches the file directly from its public host: storage.lczero.org, raw.githubusercontent.com or huggingface.co. These hosts can see your IP address and the request, under their own privacy policies. The downloads contain no personal data from you. Once downloaded, the models run offline.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
