Chessformator Support

Chessformator is an offline chess-coaching app. Everything runs on your device.

Contact

Email alpinist@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

You can also open an issue on GitHub. Please don't post personal information or full game archives there.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Analysis, coaching and voice input run on your device. The internet is only used if you choose to download an optional model (for example a Maia, Leela or Whisper network, or the Qwen coaching model) from the Models screen.

My Chessnut Move board won't connect

Turn on Bluetooth, allow Bluetooth access for Chessformator in Settings, and make sure the board is powered on and not connected to another app.

Voice input doesn't work

Allow microphone access in Settings and download a Whisper model from the Models screen. Audio is transcribed on the device and deleted afterwards.

How do I delete my data?

Your games and profile are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Reporting a bug

Please include your device model, iOS version, the app version (Settings screen) and the steps that triggered the problem.
